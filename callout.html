How to set up the shortcut (once)

Cloude writes the message; an iPhone Shortcut called Cloude Pickup sends it from your own number and email without asking. Set it up in the Shortcuts app:

  1. Open Shortcuts, tap +, and name the new shortcut exactly Cloude Pickup.
  2. Add the action Send Message. Tap Message and choose Shortcut Input. Tap Recipients and pick up to 3 people. Open the action's little arrow and turn Show When Run off.
  3. Add the action Send Email. Put Shortcut Input as the message, pick up to 3 people, and type a subject such as Pickup needed. Open its arrow and turn Show Compose Sheet off.
  4. Tap Done.

Test it first with only your own number and email as the recipients, then swap in the real people. The first time it runs, the iPhone asks whether the shortcut may send messages: choose Always Allow. If the test arrives empty, open the shortcut, tap its top line, and turn on Share Sheet.

To change who gets it, edit the shortcut in the Shortcuts app.
