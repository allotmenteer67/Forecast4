Leave the distance empty to draw a bearing line; up to three lines (A, B, C) can be drawn from different places, and the map circles where they cross. Compass points (SW, NE…) are map directions. A bearing in degrees is magnetic unless you pick °T. A 6-figure grid reference (SM 812 093) is a 100 m square, so the pin goes in its middle; 8 figures is a 10 m square. "Miles" means land miles (as someone on shore might say); at sea, use nm. A quick picture only — check positions against the coastguard's own.
